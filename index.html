THE WORKFLOW

Built for aggressive modern production.

skiQ combines surgical EQ control, channel-specific processing and visual analysis into a fast workflow designed for Trap, Drill and modern Hip-Hop.

01

16-Band Parametric EQ

Shape frequencies precisely with Low Cut, Bell, Notch and High Cut filter types.

02

Channel Control

Work directly on Mid, Side, Left or Right channels for focused frequency shaping.

03

Real-Time Analysis

Visualize the spectrum with peak and frequency information plus analyzer freeze workflow.

04

808 & Low End

Focus on sub-frequency management and low-end control when shaping heavy 808-driven productions.

05

Creative Processing

Use halftime processing and rhythmic divisions as part of the sound-design workflow.

06

DAW Workflow

VST3 support, DAW automation and a focused interface designed for fast production decisions.

ABOUT SKIQ

Frequency control as a creative instrument.

  • Mid / Side / Left / Right processing

  • Analyzer freeze
